02 · About

The Person Behind the Code

I'm a Toronto-based Computer Science student at York University with a love for all things science and technology. I'm fascinated by the universe, from distant galaxies and the possibility of life beyond Earth to the idea of humans one day living on other planets. I'm also interested in artificial intelligence, software development, and building things that people can actually use.

My curiosity doesn't stop at technology. Through Model United Nations, philosophy club, and courses like Minds, Brains and Machines, I find myself drawn to questions about humans and machines. Are we simply creating better versions of ourselves? Should we? And could intelligence, or even consciousness, exist in ways we haven't imagined yet?

I've gained industry experience at Scotiabank, where I built a productivity dashboard for the team, and worked as a Teaching Assistant at U+ Education. I'm hoping to pursue AI/ML research and development, continue my studies through a master's and PhD, and contribute to technology that makes a meaningful difference for both the people and the planet.
